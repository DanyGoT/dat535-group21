    Where is delay gained, and where is it recovered?

    Each run between two consecutive stops: the delay on arrival at the next stop minus the delay on leaving this one. Time spent at stops is left out. Each direction is drawn on its right-hand side of travel.

    Do road conditions go with late departures?

    Each timed departure is matched to the road data at its stop and aimed time: weather from the nearest road weather station within 10 km, active road situations within 500 m, and traffic on measured road sections within 500 m. Bars compare each condition with its reference within the same mode and hour of day, so rush hour does not pass for congestion. Nearby is not a cause.

    How far off was the departure time shown?

    Each timed departure compared with the departure time known 1, 5, 10, 20 and 30 minutes earlier. Leaving a minute or more before the time shown is the error that makes a traveller miss it.
