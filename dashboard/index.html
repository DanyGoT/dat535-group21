    Do road conditions go with late departures?

    Each timed departure is matched to the road data at its stop and aimed time: weather from the nearest road weather station within 10 km, active road situations within 500 m, and traffic on measured road sections within 500 m. Bars compare each condition with its reference within the same mode and hour of day, so rush hour does not pass for congestion. Nearby is not a cause.
